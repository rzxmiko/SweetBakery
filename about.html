About SweetBakery

A small family bakery in Astana. We bake cakes and desserts by hand since 2020.

Our story

It started with one birthday cake

In 2020 our chef Anna baked a cake for a friend. A week later there were ten orders, and a month later — our first small kitchen.

Today we bake every morning: cakes, cupcakes, macarons and croissants.
